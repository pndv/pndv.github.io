_This is part of exercises I took in the writing workshop at Esalen in July 2026. The idea is to describe the emotion without naming it. The reader should be able to guess._

The feeling starts in the gut gently constricting the insides as I pretend to type while putting my full attention to
the sounds around me and sense if he is still watching over my shoulders.

The gentle constriction has become tighter and tighter and there is nauseousness in my throat. My buttocks are clenched
so hard that I can feel the tightness coming up to my stomach. I can feel the sweat on my feet and my feet slipping in
the insole of my leather shoes. My body was preparing to run even as my mind knew there was nowhere to go.

_I was describing my stressful first job, working under a very toxic first person._
