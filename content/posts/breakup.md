_This is part of exercises I took in the writing workshop at Esalen in July 2026. This breakup was in 2014. This was on the \\(3^{rd}\\) day._

I am putting a book in the envelope. "The Little Prince", has a signature of my now ex-girlfriend and date when it was
gifted to me. My hands are steady but there is a volcano bubbling inside. I am angry at her, at my family, at the whole
situation.

Returning this book is not for closure. It is to hurt her. "You are insignificant!", "you don't matter!", My insides are
shouting silently. I don't need your book nor do I need the symbolism of taming foxes or other fauna. This book means
nothing to me, *you* mean nothing to me.

I drove to the post office and paid for the expedited shipment to Pavia, Italy. The sooner she gets it, the sooner I
will be able to strike her. Then, maybe then, the anger will cool down. Maybe then, after I have made her cry, she will
realise the terrible mistake it was to break up with me. Maybe then, after looking at the book, she will remember the
old times. Maybe then, she will want to get back with me...
